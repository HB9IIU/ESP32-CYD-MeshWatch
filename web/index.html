MeshWatch

A Meshtastic message monitor for the ESP32 Cheap Yellow Display (ESP32-2432S028).

Your browser can't flash devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer (Windows, macOS or Linux). Flashing only works from a secure (https) page.

Connect the CYD with a USB data cable, click Install and pick its serial port. If no port shows up, install the CH340 USB driver for your system and try again.

1. Prepare your Meshtastic radio

MeshWatch talks to your radio over WiFi, so the radio needs WiFi turned on:

  1. In the Meshtastic app or web client open Device Config → Network.
  2. Enable WiFi, enter your network name (SSID) and password, and save.
  • The radio must be ESP32-based (Heltec, T-Beam, LilyGO, Station G2…). nRF52 radios such as the RAK4631 or T-Echo have no WiFi.
  • ESP32 radios turn Bluetooth off while WiFi is on.

2. Install and set up MeshWatch

  1. Click Install MeshWatch above. For a first install, allow erasing the device.
  2. The CYD restarts and shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone to join the MeshWatch-Setup network.
  3. The setup page opens (otherwise open 192.168.4.1). Enter the same WiFi network as your radio.
  4. MeshWatch restarts, finds the radio on your network by itself and shows incoming messages.

What it shows

  • Messages – text messages heard by your radio, with sender, channel and time. The last 24 hours survive a power cut.
  • Nodes – everyone your radio knows, with last heard, hops or signal, and battery.
  • Info – your radio's hardware, firmware, battery, channel use and LoRa settings.
  • After two idle minutes a railway-style clock appears and counts new messages.
